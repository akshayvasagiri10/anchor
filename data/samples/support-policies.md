# Northwind Support Policies

## Refunds

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of the original purchase
date. Refunds are issued to the original payment method and typically settle
in five to seven business days.

Digital goods are refundable only if they have not been downloaded. If a
refund on a digital order fails, the system returns error code ERR_4417,
which means the entitlement is still active on at least one device. Support
must revoke the entitlement before retrying.

Annual subscriptions are refunded pro rata after the first 30 days. Monthly
subscriptions are not refunded mid-cycle; they are cancelled at the end of the
current period instead.

## Shipping

Standard shipping takes five to seven business days within the continental
United States. Express shipping arrives the next business day when ordered
before 2pm local time.

We do not ship to post office boxes, APO addresses, or freight forwarders.
Orders placed to those addresses are cancelled automatically and refunded in
full within 24 hours.

International shipping is available to 43 countries. Duties and import taxes
are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included in the checkout
total.

## Warranty

Hardware carries a 24-month limited warranty covering manufacturing defects.
The warranty does not cover liquid damage, cosmetic wear, or damage caused by
third-party accessories.

Warranty claims require the original order number. If the customer no longer
has it, support can look the order up by the serial number printed on the
underside of the device.

## Account security

Two-factor authentication is required for all accounts on the Business plan
and above. Recovery codes are shown exactly once at enrolment and are not
recoverable afterwards — an account locked out without recovery codes must go
through manual identity verification, which takes three business days.

Session tokens expire after 14 days of inactivity. Administrators can revoke
all active sessions for a user from the security tab.
